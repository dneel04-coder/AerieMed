## 1. Who this applies to

- **Field users** — personnel using the ResQruck mobile app during an incident or exercise.
- **Administrators** — personnel using the Command Console to coordinate incidents, view personnel locations, push files, and review reports.
- **Patients** — individuals whose care information is documented by field users through the app during an incident. Patients do not use the app directly; their information is entered by the responding field user as part of documenting care, on behalf of the deploying agency/organization.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account/profile information:** name, callsign, certification level, and role indicators (e.g. rope rescue, RT-130) you provide when creating a profile.

**Location information:** while you have location sharing enabled in the mobile app, we collect your device's precise GPS location, updated approximately every 10 seconds, along with battery level and a status indicator (e.g. Active, En Route). This is visible to other field users on the same mission and to administrators using the Command Console. Location sharing is tied to a "mission" you join or are assigned to — it is not collected when you are not actively sharing.

**Patient care information:** when a field user documents a patient encounter (MIST/SOAP report), the app collects and transmits whatever the field user enters — which may include the patient's age, sex, chief complaint, vital signs, injuries, treatments provided, and disposition. This is clinical documentation created by the field user in the course of patient care, not information we collect directly from patients.

**Certifications and documents:** license/certification images or PDFs you upload to the Cert Vault, and files administrators push to field devices as deployment orders.

**Incident and assignment records:** which incidents/missions you've joined or been assigned to, and timestamps for joining, leaving, and accepting assignments.

**What we do not collect:** we do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not sell or share your information with advertisers.

## 3. How we use this information

To operate the app's core purpose: coordinating field personnel during an incident, showing administrators a live operational picture (who's where, what's been documented, what's been pushed to whom), and maintaining a durable record of who was assigned to a given incident.

## 4. How information is shared

Information you enter is visible to other field users on the same mission (location, callsign, status) and to administrators of your organization using the Command Console (all of the above, plus patient reports and certifications). It is not shared outside your organization's deployment of the app, and not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

We use **Supabase** (a third-party cloud database/hosting provider) to store and transmit this data. Supabase acts as our data processor/infrastructure provider; it does not use your data for its own purposes.

## 5. Data retention

- **Location data** is automatically removed after approximately 7 days of inactivity, or when you explicitly leave a mission.
- **Patient reports, certifications, incident records, and profile information** are retained until deleted by an administrator or at your request (see Section 6). We do not currently have an automatic deletion schedule for these categories.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by contacting **[CONTACT EMAIL]** or your organization's administrator. Location sharing can be turned off at any time from within the app.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit (encrypted connections) and control access to the Command Console (administrator credentials). Deployment of this app is intended for authorized personnel within a single organization; it is not a public, multi-tenant consumer service.

## 8. Children's privacy

This app is intended for use by trained field personnel and administrators, not children, and is not directed at children under 13.
